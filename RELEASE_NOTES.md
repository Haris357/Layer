## ✨ Toasts that actually do things

Layer's little pop-up toasts are now interactive:

- **Update in one tap** — when a new version is ready, click **Update now** and it downloads and installs right from the toast, with a live progress bar.
- **Undo deletes** — removed a widget by mistake? Hit **Undo** to bring it back.
- **Handy follow-ups** — export your canvas → **Open folder**, quick-capture → it lands in your **Inbox**, publish a template → **View** it in the gallery.
- **Reminders & focus** — calendar events pop a toast (with a 5-minute **Snooze**), and finishing a focus session gives you a little cheer.

## 🔧 Fixes

- Toasts no longer get clipped behind the taskbar.
- Smoother toast progress / countdown animation.
