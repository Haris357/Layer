## ✨ Spaces — multiple desktops for your widgets

Set up different widget layouts and flip between them in an instant.

- Create as many **Spaces** as you like — work, focus, personal — each with its own widgets.
- Switch with **Ctrl + Shift + E**, or flick your mouse into the top-left **hot corner**.
- **Peek** at your bare desktop anytime by holding **Ctrl + Shift + `**.

## 🖊️ Freewrite in the Notes widget

A built-in freewriting mode to beat the blank page.

- Pick a length (5–20 min) and just write — **no backspace, no editing**, only forward.
- A calm full-screen view with a live word count, and a little celebration when you make it to the end.

## 🎧 Calmer focus

- **Ambient sounds** in the Pomodoro — rain, ocean, forest, fireplace, and soft noise.
- Gentle **sound effects** for the timer and the light/dark toggle.
- **Rock-solid timers** — your Pomodoro and note timers stay accurate now, even when Layer is in the background.

## 🌤️ A desktop that matches your vibe

- **Match accent to wallpaper** — Layer picks up your wallpaper's color.
- **Ambient background & weather** effects that subtly react to the sky outside.
- **Use my location** in the Weather widget for an accurate local forecast.

## 🖥️ Screensaver, leveled up

- Three distinct styles — **Ambient, Minimal, and Quote**.
- Live **weather + now-playing** on the ambient style.
