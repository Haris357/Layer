## 🖥️ Multi‑monitor, sorted

Layer now behaves properly across multiple displays.

- The **pill & widget menu bar** always sit on your **primary screen** — never floating in the empty gap between mismatched/stacked monitors.
- **Settings is now draggable** (grab the handle in the corner) and opens on your active screen instead of the gap.
- Spaces, Notifications, Quick Capture, and the command palette all open on a real screen too.
- The desktop now **auto‑extends to a monitor you plug in** — no restart needed.

## 🗄️ Three new widgets

- **Shelf** — drag any file, image, or video onto it to stash it (kept in a hidden folder), with thumbnails; open, reveal, or remove anytime.
- **Greeting** — a warm, ever‑changing greeting with your name (100+ lines, time‑of‑day aware) in Classic, Serif, or Gradient styles.
- **Disk Info** — pick any drive to see its capacity, type (SSD/HDD), filesystem, and **live read / write / active** like Task Manager.

## 🎚️ Now Playing — finally reliable

Rebuilt the media engine so it stays accurate and **self‑heals** instead of getting stuck, and can never freeze the app.

## 🧊 Stability & polish

- Hardened the whole app against freezes — **quitting from the tray is instant**, and heavy system calls can no longer make the window "not responding."
- Cleaner Settings header (drag handle + close, never overlapping the controls).
- The Stats widget's combined drives are now labelled **Storage**.

Thanks to everyone sending feedback — keep it coming. 💛
